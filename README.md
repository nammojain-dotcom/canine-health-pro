**Canine Health Pro** is a comprehensive, production-ready interactive web suite designed to provide veterinary gold-standard care guidance for dog owners and pet caregivers. It compiles physical scorecards, size-adjusted vitals diagnostics, searchable food toxicity databases, emergency triage logic, and printable forms into a lightweight, offline-capable single-page application.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🐶 **Interactive Body Condition Score (BCS):** 1–9 visual assessment tool with physical palpation cues and profile tuck guides.
- 🩺 **Dynamic Vitals Evaluator:** Physiological normal ranges adjusted for Small, Medium, Large, and Giant dog size classes.
- 💜 **Quality of Life (HHHHHMM) Calculator:** 70-point scoring system for senior and chronically ill pet management.
- 🥦 **Food Safety Database:** Fast, live-searchable index covering fatal toxins (Xylitol, Grapes, Onions), high-fat hazards, and safe treats.
- 🚨 **Emergency Triage Tool:** Interactive symptom selector for instant categorization into Critical Emergency vs. Routine Care.
- 📋 **Offline LocalStorage Logs & Form Generators:** Create, save, and print New Patient Summaries, Pet Sitter Guides, Monthly Logs, and Symptom Trackers.
- 💾 **JSON Backup & Restore:** Complete data export and import for seamless multi-device backup.
- 🖨️ **Print & PDF Optimization:** Dedicated `@media print` layout styles for clean physical printouts.

2. Go to **Settings > Pages** in your repository.
